About RxSmart

Learn more about our mission and technology.

🎯

Project Objective

RxSmart aims to bridge the communication gap between doctors and patients. Many patients — especially elderly individuals and those in rural areas — struggle to understand handwritten prescriptions. RxSmart translates medical jargon into plain language in both English and Tamil.

⚠️

Problem Statement

Over 70% of patients in Tamil Nadu do not fully understand their prescriptions. This leads to incorrect dosages, missed medicines, and preventable health complications. The problem is compounded by illegible handwriting and technical medical terminology.

✅

Benefits to Patients

  • Understand medicines without medical training
  • Reduce medication errors caused by confusion
  • Access information in Tamil for native speakers
  • Voice playback for elderly or visually impaired users
  • Keep a digital history of all prescriptions
  • Set reminders to never miss a dose
